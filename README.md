## Purpose

This repository serves as the official **public archive** of all filings, pleadings, exhibits, and advocacy documents connected to Case No. 22STUD06657.  
It exists in the interest of **transparency, accountability, and preservation of the record**.  

All filings are presented in **court-ready format** (PDF) with **editable versions** (DOCX) where applicable.  

---

## Case Summary

- **Notice to Vacate:** January 4, 2023  
- **Lockout:** January 26, 2023 (documented via Facebook Live)  
- **Issues Raised:** Default judgment without notice, alleged perjury, misuse of Ellis Act, Section 8 interference.  
- **Relief Sought:**  
  - Vacatur of default judgment (CCP §473)  
  - Injunctive relief against unlawful eviction  
  - Reinstatement of Section 8 eligibility  
  - Relocation payments under LACC §8.52.110  
  - Damages: emotional distress, relocation costs, punitive damages  
  - RICO review of enterprise misconduct
